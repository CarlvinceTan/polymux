Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Overview

Polymux has no separate hosted account to sign in to. The native app connects to an agent server you configure. The general Polymux Privacy Policy covers the product, including details retained for older integrations.

Your data

The app caches conversations on its device. Messages and attachments are sent to your configured server, which stores authoritative history and runs agents. If the server is on another computer, your data is not confined to the app device. Hosted models and configured tools can receive task content under their own terms.

Agents and connections

An agent's MCP connections belong to you and run through its server configuration. Installing a tool does not connect every account or authorise every action. Review the service, account, access scope, and approval requests. See Privacy and permissions for native-app guidance.
